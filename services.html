Services

Our Services

Saterra Energies specialises in delivering tailored solutions across a wide range of industries. Our expertise spans Oil and Gas Supply Chain Management Support, Engineering, Procurement, Logistics, Project Management and Technical Consultancy, Petroleum Storage, Terminaling and Trading, and Power Utilities and Renewable Energy. Each service is designed to meet the unique needs of our clients, ensuring efficiency, reliability, and measurable results.

01.

Oil and Gas Supply Chain Management Support

We provide comprehensive services across the oil and gas value chain, covering upstream, midstream, and downstream operations. Our capabilities include detailed engineering, scoping, Pre-FEED and FEED, compression, gas processing and storage, crude treating and transport, pipeline and SCADA, enhanced oil recovery, and petroleum product trading. These services ensure safety, efficiency, and reliable project delivery.

02.

Engineering

Saterra delivers integrated Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) services across the oil and gas value chain. Our scope includes compliant storage tanks and infrastructure, onshore pipeline construction, process and plant layout, and full installation and maintenance covering civil, mechanical, structural, electrical, instrumentation, and automation.

We also provide pipeline pigging, inspection, and storage facility maintenance to ensure reliability and operational efficiency.

03.

Procurement

Saterra provides equipment and material supply services, including valves, flanges, pumps, nozzles, seals, O-rings, bolts, and nuts. We also support heavy-duty equipment supply, installation, and maintenance such as OCTG, pipes, and subsea systems.

Our capabilities cover HSE procurement, OEM representation, inquiry and RFP management, evaluation and selection, subcontractor negotiations, and purchase order administration.

04.

Logistics

We provide specialized transportation and logistics services, including sea and air freight, barge operations, and delivery of cargo, fuel, water, and supplies to offshore rigs, platforms, and vessels. We also offer haulage for goods, materials, and petroleum products, alongside protocol, travel, and integrated logistics support to ensure safe, efficient, and timely operations across project locations.

05.

Project Management and Technical Consultancy

We provide project management and technical consultancy services covering contract and scope management, personnel oversight, and effective communication coordination. Our capabilities include earned value monitoring, capital budgeting, risk assessment and mitigation, and client engagement.

We also manage construction implementation teams and provide skilled manpower supply to ensure efficient, timely, and cost-effective project delivery.

06.

Petroleum Storage, Terminaling and Trading

We provide industry-compliant oil and gas storage tanks and related infrastructure to support upstream operations. Building on our existing assets, we leverage our storage infrastructure and international partnerships to trade middle distillates, including LPG, naphtha, HFO, AGO, and other refined products.

07.

Power Utilities and Renewable Energy

Saterra supports conventional and renewable power projects through integrated logistics, construction, and advisory services. Our scope covers gas-fired and captive power, solar, wind, and battery systems, infrastructure development, mini-grid design and commissioning, SCADA and gas control, substations, environmental permitting, and startup support.

These capabilities enable reliable, efficient, and sustainable energy solutions across project lifecycles.

We are committed to excellence and driven by a passion for creating value and fostering long-term partnerships.
